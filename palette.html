About Palette Preview

Drop in a flat SVG to see it recoloured in 2,125 curated colour palettes, with the original and recoloured artwork side by side. You can also browse palettes without an SVG. Palette Preview is free and open source under the MIT licence.

Frequently asked questions

Does my file get uploaded?
No. Your SVG is sanitised and rendered as an isolated image; nothing leaves your browser, and the page code contains no analytics.
Which files work?
Flat SVG fills, strokes and gradient stops can be recoloured. Raster images and embedded bitmaps cannot; fonts that are not installed on your device may render differently.
Where do the palettes come from?
Sanzo Wada (348), MetBrewer (56), Wes Anderson (22), ColourLovers (992) and Color Hunt (707). See NOTICE.md for licences.
Can I use the colours in Illustrator or Procreate?
Yes. Export .ase, .swatches, .gpl, CSS custom properties, a PNG swatch sheet, or the recoloured SVG and PNG.
Is it free?
Yes. It is free to use, covered by the MIT licence, and needs no account.
